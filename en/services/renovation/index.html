Conversion & renovation

Conversions often start with demolition and concrete cutting. When we do every part ourselves, you avoid coordinating several companies and the project is easier to plan.

What’s included

  • Site visit and written quote
  • In-house demolition and concrete cutting
  • Protection of stairwells and common areas
  • Information to residents when needed
  • Coordination with plumbing, electrical and ventilation
  • Ongoing cleaning and waste handling
  • Self-inspections and final inspection

What we do

  • 01

    Pipe replacement preparation

    Strip-out of bathrooms, shafts and finishes so the plumbing contractor can start straight away. We coordinate with the association and residents.

  • 02

    Loft conversions

    From cold loft to living space: demolition, openings for stairs and windows, floor structure and interior construction.

  • 03

    Tenant fit-outs

    Conversion of offices, shops and premises for new tenants – with consideration for surrounding operations.

  • 04

    Renovation

    Kitchens, bathrooms and finishes in homes – demolition, construction and coordination of installers.

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find the answer? Get in touch.

Do I need a building permit for a loft conversion?

A loft conversion may require a building permit or notification, especially if you change the facade, add roof windows or alter load-bearing structures. Check with the municipality – we help with the documents.

Can you work while our business is operating?

Often, yes. We partition off, protect against dust and schedule noisy work at times that suit you.

Can I use the ROT tax deduction?

Renovation, conversion and extension of your home may qualify for the ROT deduction on labour costs. Check the current rules and amounts with the Swedish Tax Agency.

What does pipe replacement preparation include?

Normally strip-out of bathrooms and finishes, opening of shafts and removal – so the pipe work can start on schedule. The exact scope is agreed with the association and the main contractor.
