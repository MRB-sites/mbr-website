Waste & containers

Construction and demolition waste must be sorted and delivered to the right place. We place a container or bins, collect them when full and make sure the waste reaches an approved facility.

What’s included

  • Placement and collection as agreed
  • Advice on sorting and fractions
  • Help with permits for placement on public land
  • Transport to approved facilities
  • Documentation of received waste

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find the answer? Get in touch.

Can I put the container on the street?

On your own property, no permit is normally needed. If the container will stand on a street, pavement or other public place, a permit from the police or the municipality is usually required. We help you with the application.

What must not go in the container?

Hazardous waste such as paint, chemicals, electronics, asbestos and pressure-treated wood must be kept separate. Ask us if you are unsure.

Why does construction waste have to be sorted?

Under the Swedish Waste Ordinance, construction and demolition waste must be sorted into several fractions, including wood, mineral, metal, glass, plastic and plaster. This means more can be recycled, and sorted waste is often cheaper to deliver.
