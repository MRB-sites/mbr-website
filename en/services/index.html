The whole chain

How the services fit together

No need to coordinate several companies. We handle every step and hand over to the next without gaps.

  1. 1

    Demolition permit

    We prepare the documents and deal with the municipality.

  2. 2

    Environmental survey

    We map hazardous materials and what can be reused.

  3. 3

    Remediation

    Asbestos, PCB and other hazardous materials are removed first.

  4. 4

    Demolition

    Total, selective or interior – in the right order and safely.

  5. 5

    Sorting & recycling

    Material is sorted on site and taken to approved facilities.

  6. 6

    New construction

    We build new or rebuild on the same site.

  7. 7

    Final inspection

    Inspection, documentation and handover to you.
