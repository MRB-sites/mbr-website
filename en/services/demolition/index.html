Demolition

We demolish everything from a bathroom to an entire building. Before we start, we survey hazardous materials, and during demolition we sort material on site so that as much as possible can be reused or recycled. You have one contact from quote to cleared site.

What’s included

  • Site visit and written quote
  • Help with demolition permit or notification
  • Hazardous material survey
  • Remediation when needed
  • Barriers, protection and dust control
  • Waste sorting on site
  • Container and transport to approved facilities
  • Waste and receipt documentation
  • Clean site at handover

What we do

  • 01

    Total demolition

    The whole building is taken down – with or without the foundation. We handle permits, remediation, demolition, sorting and removal.

  • 02

    Heavy demolition

    Demolition of concrete, frames and load-bearing structures with machines suited to the job.

  • 03

    Selective demolition

    We dismantle building parts in order so they can be reused or recycled, instead of mixing everything.

  • 04

    Interior demolition

    Walls, floors, ceilings and installations ahead of a conversion – with dust protection where needed.

  • 05

    ROT demolition (bathroom, kitchen)

    Bathroom and kitchen strip-out in houses and apartments before renovation. The labour cost may qualify for the Swedish ROT tax deduction.

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find the answer? Get in touch.

Do I need a demolition permit?

Often, yes. Within an area covered by a detailed development plan, a demolition permit is normally required to demolish a building or part of one, and outside such areas a demolition notification may be required. The municipal building committee decides. We prepare the documents and handle contact with the municipality.

How long does demolition take?

It depends on size, materials and whether remediation is needed. A bathroom often takes a few days, a whole building longer. The quote includes a schedule.

Can I use the ROT tax deduction?

Demolition that is part of renovating your home, for example before a new bathroom or kitchen, may qualify for the ROT deduction on labour costs. Demolishing an entire building normally does not. Check the current rules with the Swedish Tax Agency (Skatteverket) – we itemise the labour cost on the invoice.

What happens to the demolition material?

We sort it on site into separate fractions and take it to approved facilities. Anything that can be reused is salvaged. You get documentation of what was delivered and where.

How is asbestos handled?

If the survey finds asbestos, it is removed before demolition starts, by trained staff following the Swedish Work Environment Authority’s regulations. Read more on the remediation page.
