Concrete cutting

Need a new doorway, a ventilation shaft or a hole for pipes? We saw and drill concrete, brick and aerated concrete with straight cuts and as little dust and vibration as possible.

What’s included

  • Site visit and review of drawings
  • Check for hidden installations before cutting
  • Coordination with a structural engineer for load-bearing structures
  • Temporary propping when needed
  • Water collection and dust protection
  • Removal of cut material
  • Cleaning after the job

What we do

  • 01

    Openings

    Openings for doors, windows, stairs and shafts in walls and floor slabs.

  • 02

    Wall sawing

    Straight, precise cuts in concrete walls – even in thick structures.

  • 03

    Floor sawing

    Sawing in floors and slabs, for example for drains, ducts or removing slabs.

  • 04

    Core drilling in concrete

    Round holes for pipes, ventilation and electrics in various diameters and depths.

How it works

  1. 1

    Step 1

    Site visit

    We look at the structure and take measurements.

  2. 2

    Step 2

    Documentation

    Work on load-bearing structures requires input from a structural engineer. We help you get it.

  3. 3

    Step 3

    Preparation

    We mark out, protect the surroundings and prop when needed.

  4. 4

    Step 4

    Sawing and drilling

    Cuts and holes are made to measurements and drawings.

  5. 5

    Step 5

    Removal and cleaning

    Cut material is taken away and the area is left clean.

What we need from you

  • Drawings and structural drawings, if available
  • Dimensions and position of the opening
  • Whether the wall or slab is load-bearing
  • Access to power and water
  • Contact at the housing association or property owner for apartments

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find the answer? Get in touch.

Do I need a permit or notification for a new opening?

Work on a load-bearing structure normally requires a notification to the municipality and documentation from a structural engineer. In a housing cooperative apartment you also need the board’s permission. We help you work out what applies.

Will there be a lot of dust?

We usually saw and drill with water, which binds the dust. We protect surrounding surfaces and collect the water.

Can you cut openings in a cooperative apartment?

Yes. We coordinate with the association, plan the work to disturb neighbours as little as possible and keep to the association’s working hours.
