Circularity, not mountains of waste

Demolition produces a lot of material. We make sure as much as possible is used again.

  • Reuse first

    Building parts in good condition are salvaged before demolition starts.

  • Sorting on site

    Wood, mineral, metal, glass, plastic and plaster are kept apart from the start.

  • Minimal environmental impact

    Dust control, planned transport and correct handling of hazardous waste.
