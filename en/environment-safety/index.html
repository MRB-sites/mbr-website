Environment & safety

The right material in the right place – and everyone home safe

Demolition and remediation affect both the environment and the people doing the work. That’s why we have clear routines for both.

Waste

How we sort and recycle

Construction and demolition waste must be sorted into fractions on site. This is how we do it.

  1. 1

    Step 1

    Survey

    We identify hazardous waste and materials that can be reused.

  2. 2

    Step 2

    Selective dismantling

    Anything reusable is carefully dismantled before the rest is demolished.

  3. 3

    Step 3

    Sorting on site

    At least wood, mineral, metal, glass, plastic and plaster are kept apart, as required by the Waste Ordinance.

  4. 4

    Step 4

    Transport

    Waste is taken to approved facilities.

  5. 5

    Step 5

    Documentation

    You get records of quantities, fractions and facilities.

Reuse

Reuse before recycling

The best waste is the waste that never becomes waste. Where possible we salvage building parts and materials that can be used again.

  • Building parts and materials that can be used again are identified before demolition starts.
  • Reusable items are carefully dismantled instead of demolished.
  • What cannot be reused is sorted on site for recycling.

Documentation

What you receive as a customer

Documents for your inspection plan and your own environmental work.

  • Material and waste management plan
  • Hazardous waste survey
  • Transport documents and receipts
  • Summary of quantities per fraction
  • Remediation documentation (asbestos/PCB) where relevant
  • Documents for the inspection plan and final consultation

Work environment

Safety routines

Safety is planned before the first machine starts.

  • Risk assessment

    Every job starts with a risk assessment and a plan for doing the work safely.

  • Protective equipment

    The right protective equipment for every task – respiratory and hearing protection, helmets and protective clothing.

  • Staff register

    A staff register is kept on our construction sites.

  • Barriers and protection

    The work area is fenced off and the surroundings protected from dust, noise and vibration.
