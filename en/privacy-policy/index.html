Privacy policy

This describes how Malmö Bygg & Riv AB processes personal data when you contact us, request a quote or apply for a job. The template must be reviewed and completed before publication.

Data controller

Malmö Bygg & Riv AB, corporate ID no. 559097-5214, Von Lingens Väg 122, 213 71 Malmö, is the data controller. Questions about personal data can be sent to info@mbr.se.

What data we process

We process the data you provide to us:

  • Quote requests: name, company/housing association, email, phone, property address, description of the job and any photos and drawings you attach.
  • Job applications: name, contact details, CV and the information you write in your application.
  • Contact by email and phone: the information included in the contact.

Purposes and legal basis

  • Answering requests and providing quotes – steps prior to entering a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Carrying out jobs and contracts – contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Bookkeeping and invoicing – legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)), Swedish Bookkeeping Act.
  • Recruitment – consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give when you submit your application.

How long we keep data

  • Quote requests that do not lead to a job are deleted when they are no longer needed to answer the request.
  • Job applications are deleted when the recruitment has ended, unless you consent to us keeping them longer.
  • Accounting records: seven years under the Swedish Bookkeeping Act.

Recipients and processors

We never sell your data. We use suppliers that process data on our behalf:

  • A form service that receives what you send through the website’s forms.
  • A web host that runs the website.
  • An email service we use to reply to you.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, rectification or erasure of your data, restriction of processing, to object to processing and to receive your data (data portability). If you have given consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Contact us at info@mbr.se.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.
