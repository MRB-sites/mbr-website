About us

The Malmö contractor that handles the whole chain

Demolition, remediation, concrete cutting and construction – in one company.

Our story

Malmö Bygg & Riv AB is a construction contractor from Malmö.

We are a local contractor that demolishes, remediates, cuts concrete and builds new. The idea is simple: you shouldn’t have to coordinate five different companies to turn an old building into a new one.

What we stand for

  • 01

    Responsibility

    We take responsibility for the whole chain – for the work, for the material and for how it affects the surroundings.

  • 02

    Safety

    No schedule is more important than everyone going home unharmed. We plan for safety before we start.

  • 03

    Reliability

    We keep our promises: price, time and a contact person who answers.

Want to work with us?

We’re looking for people who want to work safely and do a proper job.
